Listen to Earth

A Debussy piano piece thins out as Arctic sea ice shrinks: the notes that play are decided by 48 years of NASA-hosted NSIDC data, and you can hear the same phrase in two different years.

▶ Press to listen (about 1 minute)

Challenge

NASA Space Apps 2026 · The Earth Information Jukebox. Earth data is shown mostly as images; this turns it into sound so blind and low-vision people can explore it, and anyone can feel it.

Demo

30-second demo with English captions burned in (draft: music, voice and visuals will be revised before submission).

How the data becomes sound

DataSound
September Arctic sea ice extent (million km²), one value per year, fixed scale 3–8How much of the piano plays: ≥5% top melody line, ≥30% + bass, above that the inner voices return one by one
The same numberA sine tone on a fixed D-dorian scale (D4–D6): higher = more ice
NarrationLowers the music volume only; never changes which notes play

Which notes exist, their pitch and rhythm are Debussy's. The data decides which of them are played. We do not claim that every note comes from data.

What the data can and cannot say

One September value per year, so this is a long-term comparison, not a seasonal cycle. Largest in this record: 1980 (7.67 million km², 20% above the 1981–2010 mean of 6.41). Smallest: 2012 (3.57, 44% below). It shows a decline since 1979 with large year-to-year swings; it does not explain causes and a single year can rebound.

Evidence

Listening test: not yet run. The test is ready: listening-test.html (narration off, picture hidden, pairs of the same phrase, answer "which has more ice"). We will report: how many listeners, whether they could tell more ice from less ice with narration off, picture hidden and numbers hidden, and where they struggled. No claim about blind or low-vision users is made until blind or low-vision listeners have tried it.

Accessibility built in: keyboard operation, screen-reader text, a "read this year's data" button, a "why does it sound like this" button, vibration cues on Android, English and Chinese text.

Sources, licences, AI use

Data: NSIDC Sea Ice Index v4.0 (NOAA@NSIDC, G02135), Northern Hemisphere monthly extent, September file. Baseline: mean of 1981–2010 values in the same file. Music: Debussy, public-domain scores; MIDI from the Mutopia Project. Piano: Salamander Grand Piano samples (CC-BY, Alexander Holm). Libraries: Tone.js and @tonejs/midi (MIT). Narration: synthetic generic voice (VoxCPM2, Apache-2.0), not a clone of any person; the page falls back to the browser's built-in voice if the file is missing.
